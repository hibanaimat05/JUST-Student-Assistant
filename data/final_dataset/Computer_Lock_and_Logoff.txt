Computer Lock and Log Off

Recommended Security Practices:

1. Lock your computer whenever you walk away.
2. Enable automatic locking after a short period of inactivity.
3. Log off your computer and applications when you plan to be away for a longer period.
4. Shut down your computer when it is not in use to avoid being hacked.
