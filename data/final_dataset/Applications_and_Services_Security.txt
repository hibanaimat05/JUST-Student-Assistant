Applications and Services Security

Recommended Security Practices:

1. Delete applications that you do not use. Unused applications are rarely patched, and existing vulnerabilities may be exploited.
2. Disable nonessential services running on your computer.
3. Pay special attention to:
   - File Sharing
   - Print Sharing
