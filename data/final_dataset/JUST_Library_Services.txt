JUST Library Services

The JUST Library provides printed and electronic information resources
for students and university staff.

Services and resources mentioned in the source:
- Printed books and electronic books.
- Scientific journals and electronic databases.
- Access to library resources from inside and outside the university.
- Electronic Search Discovery (EDS) unified search index.
- Computer laboratories.
- Wireless internet access inside the library.
- Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) self-service for borrowing
  and returning library materials.
- Document Delivery System (DDS).
- Inter-Library Loan (ILL) service.
- Digital Library Assistant (DLA).

The source states that the library collection includes:
- 195,000 books.
- 50,000 volumes of back issues of scientific journals.
- 590,000 subscribed eBooks.
- Access to 85 databases containing approximately 156,000
  full-text electronic journals.

Library website:
http://www.just.edu.jo/library
