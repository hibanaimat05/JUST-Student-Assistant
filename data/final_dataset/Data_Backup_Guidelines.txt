Data Backup Guidelines

Regularly back up your computer data.

Why Backups Are Important:
Computer hardware may fail. Data may also be deleted accidentally or maliciously by malware or an intruder.

Recommended Backup Practices:
1. Perform a full system backup.
2. Follow it with regular incremental backups.
3. Create a backup before making any changes to your computer.
