JUST - Department of Student Services

Main services:
- University loans for eligible students.
- Scholarships for outstanding students.
- Student employment opportunities in university departments, colleges, and laboratories.
- Electronic student records and personal information services.
- Student ID issuance and replacement.
- Lost-and-found services.
- Student health insurance.
- Student-related documents, including conduct and health insurance documents.
- Support for students with disabilities through the Access and Inclusive Education Platform.

Student employment:
The source states that students may work up to 15 hours per week.
The wage details extracted from the source require verification before being provided as current information.

Student IDs:
The source describes smart university ID issuance and fees for lost or damaged cards.
The exact fees should be verified before use.

Health insurance:
The source states that registered students are medically insured.
It mentions the university health center and King Abdullah University Hospital.
For emergencies, it mentions nearby health centers or government hospitals, with a treatment receipt submitted to the university.

Access and Inclusive Education Platform:
- Support for inclusive academic, administrative, health, and extracurricular services.
- Assessment of accessibility in campus buildings.
- Assistance accessing university facilities.
- Reasonable academic accommodations in lectures and laboratories.
- Accommodations for theoretical and practical examinations.
- Support for participation in extracurricular activities.
- Coordination with college liaison officers.

Smoking Cessation Clinic:
- Awareness of the risks of different forms of smoking.
- Lectures and seminars about quitting smoking.
- Community awareness activities.
- Student activities related to combating smoking.
- Counseling for students and employees who wish to quit tobacco and smoking products.
- Volunteer opportunities related to smoking prevention.
