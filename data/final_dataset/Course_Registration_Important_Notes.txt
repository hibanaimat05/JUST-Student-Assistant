JUST Course Registration - Important Notes

1. Official Registration Website

The university states that this is the only official website for course registration. The university is not responsible for any other website used for student registration.

English Registration Link:
https://services.just.edu.jo/sturegistration/Login_En.aspx

2. Synchronous Courses

- A synchronous course does not appear among the available courses if the student has not studied the original course or has not registered for it in the same registration semester.
- A student who wants to register two synchronous courses must first register for the original theoretical course during the course registration process.
- After registering the original theoretical course, the system allows the student to register for the synchronous practical course.

3. Study Plan

Students must fully comply with the courses included in the study plan issued by their academic department.

4. Student Handbook

Students should always refer to the instructions in the Student Handbook and follow them.

5. Problems During Course Registration

If a student encounters a problem during course registration, they should contact:
- Their registrar in the Admission and Registration Unit.
- Their academic mentor in their department or college.
