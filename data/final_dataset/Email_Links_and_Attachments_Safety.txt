Links and Attachments in Email Messages

Recommended Security Practices:

1. Do not click links or open attachments in email messages received from known or unknown senders.
2. Hover your mouse over a link to check whether the visible URL is an alias for the actual URL.
3. Copy and paste the URL into your web browser.
4. Check with the sender before opening unsolicited attachments.
5. Do not assume that an email was sent by someone you know just because their name appears in the FROM field.
