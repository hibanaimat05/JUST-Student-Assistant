JUST Student Financial Aid and Work-Study

Financial aid opportunities mentioned in the source include:
- University loans.
- Work-study program.

The work-study program provides eligible students with part-time
employment opportunities on campus.

Hourly wages stated in the source:
- Undergraduate students: JD 1.0 per hour.
- Graduate students: JD 1.5 per hour.

Major campus employers mentioned:
- Deanship of Student Affairs.
- JUST Library.
- Students' Cafeteria.
- Faculties.

Source: JUST Student Life page.
