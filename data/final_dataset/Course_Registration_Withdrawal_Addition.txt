JUST Course Registration, Withdrawal, and Addition Guide

1. Accessing the Registration System

Students can access course registration through:
JUST Website > Students > Course Registration

Direct link:
https://services.just.edu.jo/sturegistration

Students must:
- Enter their university ID and password.
- Complete the identity verification (I'm not a robot).
- Access the system during their assigned registration time.
- Check their registration appointment through the Student Services page.

2. Registering Courses

Students can register courses by:
- Entering the course line number and section number.
- Selecting courses from the available courses for registration.

3. Deleting a Course

- Click the Delete option next to the course.
- The total registered credit hours must not fall below 9 credit hours.
- Students are advised not to delete courses that are prerequisites for other courses to avoid delaying their academic plans.
- The academic plan can be checked through Student Services > Registration > Academic Plan.

4. Changing a Course Section

- Click the Change Section option.
- Select an available section and its offered time.
- Alternatively, enter the section number directly.
- Click Save next to the course to confirm the change.

5. Finding a Course Line Number

If students do not know the course line number, they can consult their indicative academic plan through the Student Services platform.

6. Checking Open Course Sections

Students can visit the university website and open the Study Schedule.
Select the college and department offering the course to view the department courses and section status.

7. Registration Rules and Restrictions

- Students may access the registration website only during their assigned registration period.
- The withdrawal and addition period begins after the final registration period ends.
- Annual-system students in Doctor of Medicine and Dentistry programs must follow the indicative schedule and cannot delete any scientific course.
- The maximum undergraduate study load in the first and second semesters is 18 credit hours.
- The minimum study load is 9 credit hours.
- The maximum undergraduate study load in the summer semester is 10 credit hours.
- Students must pay the required university fees before accessing registration for the courses they wish to register.

8. Additional Instructions

- The source states that not using the logout link after registration results in a five-minute waiting period before the next login.
- Students are asked to provide their mobile phone number to receive important information and dates through WhatsApp.
- The source includes a special notice for newly admitted Nursing students holding a three-year Nursing diploma, but the extracted text does not provide the complete instruction.
