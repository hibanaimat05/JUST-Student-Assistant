
JUST Students Application

The JUST Students application is the official mobile application
for students of Jordan University of Science and Technology.

It is developed by the university's Information Technology and
Communications Center team.

The application supports both Arabic and English languages.

Note: The available source does not provide the complete list
of application services.


Moodle Mobile Application

The Moodle mobile application allows students to access their
Moodle Learning Management System courses anytime and anywhere.

Students can browse course content even without an internet connection.

Note: These details describe the general Moodle application.
They do not confirm specific JUST configuration or procedures.
