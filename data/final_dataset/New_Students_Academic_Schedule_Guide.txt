JUST New Students Academic Schedule Guide

1. Viewing the Academic Schedule

New students can view their academic schedule through:

JUST Website > Students > Course Registration

Direct link:
https://services.just.edu.jo/sturegistration

The university automatically builds the academic schedule for newly admitted students. Students do not need to visit the Admission and Registration Unit for this process.

Important condition:
Students must have taken the placement examinations or have an approved placement examination equivalency from the relevant dean.

2. Accessing the Registration Page

Students must:
- Enter their university ID and password correctly.
- Complete identity verification by selecting "I'm not a robot".
- Enter their mobile phone number when requested.
- Access the course registration page to view registered courses and credit hours.

3. Checking Remaining Fees and Electronic Payment

Students can select "Electronic Payment Number and Required Fees" to:

- Check the remaining fees for registered credit hours.
- Enter the number of credit hours they wish to retain to calculate the remaining amount due.
- View the electronic payment number used for eFAWATEERcom.

Payment methods mentioned in the source:

A. eFAWATEERcom:
- University: Jordan University of Science and Technology.
- Service: Deposit into the student's balance.

B. Payment through any branch of Cairo Amman Bank.

The electronic payment number shown in the source is an illustrative example and must not be treated as a general student payment number.

4. Deleting a Course

- Students can delete a course using the Delete option next to it.
- The registered credit hours must not fall below 9.
- Students are advised not to delete courses that are prerequisites for other courses, to avoid delaying their academic plans.

5. Changing a Course Section

- Select the Change Section option.
- Choose an available section and its offered time.
- Alternatively, enter the section number directly.
- Save the change next to the course.

6. Registering University Electives or Previously Deleted Courses

To register a university elective or a course previously deleted from the indicative academic plan:

- Enter the course line number in the line number field at the top of the page.
- Click Open Sections, or enter the section number directly.
- Complete the "I'm not a robot" verification.
- Click Register.

7. Finding University Elective Courses

Students can find university elective courses through:

Student Services > Registration > Academic Plan.

8. Finding a Deleted Course Line Number

If a student does not know the line number of a previously deleted course, they can consult the indicative academic plan through the Student Services platform.

9. Checking Open Course Sections

Students can browse open course sections through the university Study Schedule page.

Select the college and department offering the course to view its courses and section status.

10. Special Registration Rules

Annual-System Students:
- Students in Doctor of Medicine and Dentistry programs must follow the indicative academic schedule.
- They are not allowed to delete scientific courses.
- They can only change the course time.

Courses Outside the Indicative Plan:
- Students cannot add courses outside their indicative academic plan, except for university elective courses.
- The study load must not exceed 18 credit hours.

Schedule Modification and Fees:
- The source states that the schedule can only be modified after paying the fee difference or meeting the stated payment requirement for the credit hours to be retained.
- The exact wording of this payment condition in the extracted source is unclear.

Bridging Students:
- Bridging students register their courses themselves through the registration page.
- They can consult the department chair or college to identify available courses before course equivalency is completed.

11. Additional Notice

The source includes a special notice for newly admitted Nursing students holding a three-year Nursing diploma, but the exact course instruction is not sufficiently clear in the extracted text.
