JUST Student Cafeteria Services

- The university has three student cafeterias.
- They provide hot meals, sandwiches, snacks, hot drinks, and soft drinks.
- The source states that typical costs range between 350 and 1500 fils.
- Breakfast is also served.

Opening hours stated in the source:
Sunday to Thursday, 7:30 AM to 3:30 PM.

Source: JUST Student Life page.


Affordable Food Services

JUST aims to provide students with affordable and nutritious meals
and to support equitable access to food within the university community.

The university works to negotiate competitive prices with food
providers and maintain costs below those in surrounding urban areas.
It also raises awareness about food insecurity and provides resources
to help students manage their budgets and make informed dietary choices.

On-Campus Food Services

1. Students Cafeteria:
- Serves both students and employees.
- Offers affordable breakfast and lunch options.
- Food includes falafel, hummus, traditional dishes such as mansaf,
  burgers, and hot dogs.

2. Almla'eb Cafeteria:
- Focuses on healthy food options.
- Includes fresh vegetables and nutritious ingredients.
- Offers desserts such as cheesecake and brownies.
- Provides freshly squeezed juices.

3. Buildings Cafeteria:
- Located in the classroom building, opposite the library.
- Offers various dishes at reasonable prices.

Examples of Meals and Prices (as stated in the source)

- Mansaf: $1.41 USD. Contents: Rice, Indian Kabab.
- Grilled: $2.40 USD. Contents: Grilled items.
- Kabseh: $2.12 USD. Contents: Rice, Beans.
- Kibbeh: $1.69 USD. Contents: Kibbeh.
- Maglobeh: $2.12 USD. Contents: Rice, Chicken.

Currency Note:
The source states that 1 JD is approximately equal to 1.41 USD.
Prices are preserved as displayed in the source.

Food Services Department - Responsibilities

The Department of Catering and Food Services is responsible for:

1. Providing food services at university cafeterias for students
   and employees.
2. Providing food catering services for conferences, workshops,
   and training courses held at the university.
3. Providing accommodation and appropriate furniture for faculty
   members and university guests.
4. Following up on residence-related matters at the University
   Housing Complex.

Department Sections

The Food Services Department performs its duties through four sections:

1. Food and Beverage Production Section.
2. Cleaning Services Section.
3. Food Catering Services Section.
4. Faculty Members and Guests Residence Section.
