JUST - Student Housing Services

Internal services:
- Seven kitchens in each building, equipped with cooking and food-storage facilities.
- Study halls on each floor.
- Lounges with TV and satellite.
- Reception rooms for families.
- Prayer rooms.
- Laundry rooms with automatic washers and dryers.
- Heating during winter and hot water throughout the year.
- Wireless internet in student rooms and common areas.
- Cleaning staff for shared spaces.
- Air-conditioned studio rooms in Building B4 and Princess Basma Residence.
- Security cameras at building entrances.

External services:
- Shuttle buses between residence buildings and university faculties.
- Daily free shuttle service between student housing and Irbid after class hours.
- Commercial complex with cafeteria, dry cleaning, bookstore, mini-market, and salon.
- Healthcare services: daytime campus clinics and nighttime emergency and ambulance services through King Abdullah University Hospital.
- Round-the-clock maintenance services.

Student life:
- Recreational trips.
- Sports events.
- Cultural and educational activities, workshops, competitions, and exhibitions.
- Supervisors available 24/7 for psychological, social, and academic counseling.

Student Housing Application Procedure

Students can apply for on-campus housing electronically
through the Student Services page.

Application Link:
https://services.just.edu.jo/stuservices

Application Steps:
1. Open the Student Services page using the link above.
2. Select "Student Housing Registration".
3. Enter the required information in the on-campus
   student housing registration form.
4. Click "Save Application".
5. Click "Approve Application" to submit it for review.
6. A message will be sent to the student's university email
   with the application acceptance and details about
   completing the remaining procedures.

Student Housing - General Information

The university provides on-campus accommodation for students
coming from:
- Arab countries.
- Islamic countries.
- Friendly countries.
- Different governorates within Jordan.

Objectives of Student Housing:
- Strengthening relationships among students.
- Encouraging students to live together and cooperate.
- Providing a comfortable home-like living environment.
- Providing services needed by students in general.
- Giving particular attention to the needs of students with disabilities.

Housing Regulations:
The Student Housing Department applies specific regulations
and instructions that residents, including students with disabilities,
are expected to observe and follow.
