JUST - Student Services Password Reset

The supplied JUST guide explains how students can obtain a temporary
password if they forget their Student Services password.

Steps:

1. Open the Student Services login page.
2. Click the Forgot Password link.
3. Enter the student serial number and date of birth.
4. Select the I'm not a robot checkbox.
5. A notification indicates that a link for creating and activating
   a temporary password has been sent.
6. Open the university email account or the alternate email account.
7. Look for a message from webmaster@just.edu.jo.
8. Open the link in the message.
9. The temporary password creation page will appear.
10. Click the button to create a new temporary password.
11. Return to the Student Services main page.
12. Choose Change Password.
13. Enter the student serial number, temporary password, and new
    password twice.
14. Follow the password requirements shown on the page.

The guide also mentions visiting the Public Service Office at D1L0
if assistance is needed.
