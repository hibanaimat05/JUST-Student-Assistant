Student Clubs at JUST

The Department of Student Activities and Initiatives supervises
student clubs formed on campus according to specific instructions.

Objectives of Student Clubs:
1. Encourage voluntary work, public service, and social work.
2. Develop cultural, social, sports, and artistic talents and hobbies.
3. Provide students with opportunities to participate in activities
   that match their interests, abilities, and capabilities.
4. Enable non-Jordanian students to participate and express their
   cultures, interests, and skills.
5. Develop interest in aesthetic and artistic activities.
6. Promote cultural heritage in Jordan, the Arab world, and the
   Islamic world, as well as local and international culture.
7. Organize cultural, social, and artistic activities, including
   art exhibitions and various celebrations, with approval from
   the Deanship of Student Affairs.

Student Club Elections:
- Administrative boards of student clubs are elected on campus.
- Elections take place during the first semester of each year.
- Voting requires participation by 51% of the active members
  of each club.
- The Deanship of Student Affairs arranges and supervises
  the election procedures.

الأنشطة الرياضية داخل الحرم الجامعي

توفر الجامعة للطلبة إمكانية ممارسة الأنشطة الرياضية داخل الحرم الجامعي من خلال دائرة النشاط الرياضي، التابعة لعمادة شؤون الطلبة.

تشمل المرافق الرياضية:
- ملعب كرة القدم.
- صالة رياضية تحتوي على ألعاب رياضية متعددة الأغراض.
- بركة سباحة.
- مركز للياقة البدنية مجهز بمجموعة من الأجهزة الرياضية.
- غرفة ساونا وغرفة بخار.

يشرف على المرافق والبرامج التدريبية مدربون متخصصون من ذوي الخبرة والكفاءة، ويقدمون التدريب والنصائح والإرشادات الرياضية للمشاركين.
