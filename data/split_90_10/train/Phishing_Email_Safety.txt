Phishing Email Safety

Recommended Security Practices:

1. Do not respond to email requests for your username, password, or personal information.
2. JUST will never send an email requesting your JUST username and password.
3. Do not provide your username, email address, or password on any website reached through a link in an email message.
4. Use common sense. If an email looks suspicious, it is probably malicious.
