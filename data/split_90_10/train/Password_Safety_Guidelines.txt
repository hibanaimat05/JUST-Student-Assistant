Password Safety Guidelines

Recommended Password Security Practices:

1. Create strong passwords.
2. Use a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
3. Use longer passwords, as more characters make a password stronger.
4. Do not use dictionary words.
5. When changing your password, make the new password unique and avoid reusing parts of the old password.
6. Do not use the same password for all accounts.
7. Never share your passwords.
8. Do not store passwords in plain text.
9. Physically protect any written passwords.
10. Do not log in to an untrusted computer using your JUST account credentials.
