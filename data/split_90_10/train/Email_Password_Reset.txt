JUST - Self-Service Password Reset (SSPR)

SSPR stands for Self-Service Password Reset.
It allows users to reset their passwords without contacting IT staff.

The supplied JUST guide describes the following activation steps:

1. Sign in to the university email account.
2. When the setup page appears, click Next.
3. Configure an alternate verification method.

Phone verification:
- Choose the country code.
- Enter your mobile phone number.
- Select Text or Call to receive a verification code.
- Enter the code and click Verify.

Alternate email verification:
- Enter an active external email address.
- Click Email me.
- Retrieve the verification code from that email.
- Enter the code and click Verify.
- Finish the setup.
