Sensitive Data Protection

Recommended Security Practices:

1. Do not store unencrypted sensitive data on your computer or digital devices.
   Examples include:
   - Social security numbers
   - Passwords
   - Credit card numbers
   - Research data

2. Mobile devices and storage media can be easily stolen. Sensitive data stored on them may be used for criminal activities.

3. Make sure sensitive data is encrypted in transit.

4. Useful free encryption applications are available on the Internet.

5. If you are not required to retain sensitive data, purge it from your computer.
