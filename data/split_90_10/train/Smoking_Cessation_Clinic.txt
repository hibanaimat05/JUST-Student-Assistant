JUST Smoking Cessation Clinic

Official Website:
https://www.just.edu.jo/WellnessClinics/SmokingCessationClinic/Pages/default.aspx

Vision:
Towards a smoke-free university community.

Mission:
To contribute to reducing smoking rates within the university by:
- Providing scientific awareness about the harms of tobacco
  and smoking in all its forms.
- Assisting people who wish to quit smoking.
- Offering the necessary medical advice.

Clinic Objectives:
1. Spreading awareness about smoking in all its forms.
2. Organizing lectures and seminars by specialists about
   the importance of quitting smoking from health,
   psychological, financial, and religious perspectives.
3. Participating in raising community awareness, especially
   among school students, about the dangers of smoking.
4. Organizing regular activities to highlight the role of
   students in combating smoking.
5. Providing accurate medical advice to employees and students
   who wish to quit tobacco products and smoking.

Awareness Activities:
- Lectures and seminars.
- Community awareness activities.
- Student awareness initiatives.
- Events and workshops.
- Participation in new-student orientation activities.
