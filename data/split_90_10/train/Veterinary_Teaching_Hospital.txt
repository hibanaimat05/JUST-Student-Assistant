
JUST Veterinary Teaching Hospital (VTH)

General Overview:
The Veterinary Teaching Hospital at Jordan University of
Science and Technology serves as the clinical and training arm
of the Faculty of Veterinary Medicine.

It provides comprehensive veterinary services, practical
education, and research opportunities in animal health and welfare.

The hospital serves:
- Small animals.
- Large animals.
- Equines (horses).

Specialized Sections:
1. Small Animal Medicine and Surgery.
2. Large Animal and Equine Services.
3. Diagnostic Imaging.
4. Clinical Laboratories.
5. Pharmacy.
6. Postmortem Facilities.

Education and Training:
- Hands-on clinical training for undergraduate veterinary students.
- Training for postgraduate veterinary students.
- Training for interns and residents.
- Development of diagnostic skills, critical thinking,
  and ethical veterinary practice.

Community Services:
The hospital provides preventive, diagnostic, and therapeutic
services to:
- Farmers.
- Animal owners.
- Public institutions across Jordan.

Research:
The hospital supports scientific research and collaborative
projects in:
- Animal health.
- Epidemiology.
- Surgery.
- Pharmacology.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

General Information

Services:
The VTH provides comprehensive veterinary care for small animals,
large animals, and equines, including:
- General consultations.
- Surgery.
- Internal medicine.
- Diagnostics.
- Postmortem services.
- A fully equipped pharmacy.

Location:
The hospital is located within the main campus of JUST
in Irbid, Jordan.

Working Hours:
Sunday to Thursday, 8:30 AM to 4:00 PM.
For urgent cases, contact the hospital directly.

Appointments and Admissions

Booking an Appointment:
Appointments can be scheduled:
- Online through the Book an Appointment page.
- By contacting hospital reception.

Information Required for Booking:
- Animal owner's name and contact details.
- Animal species, breed, and age.
- Relevant medical history and reason for consultation.
- Previous medical records or diagnostic reports, if available.

Animals Served:
- Dogs.
- Cats.
- Small mammals.
- Horses.
- Livestock.

Specialized care for other species may be available upon request.
Contact the hospital for details.

Medical Records:
Registered clients can request access to their pets' medical records:
- Through hospital reception.
- Online through the secure Patient Portal.

Education and Student Opportunities

- Clinical rotations for JUST veterinary students.
- Practical clinical training.
- Research opportunities.
- Faculty-led and independent research projects.
- Clinical trials, laboratory studies, and fieldwork.

Feedback and Support:
Feedback can be submitted through the Feedback Form
on the Contact Us page or directly by email to hospital administration.
