
JUST Youth Friendly Clinic

General Description:
The Youth Friendly Clinic provides free services to JUST students
in the areas of sexual and reproductive health and psychosocial support.

Services are provided through:
- One-to-one sessions.
- Group activities.

Vision:
Achieving students' optimal physical and psychological health
and well-being to excel in their academic and social life.
The clinic also promotes students' sense of belonging by serving
their peers and communities.

Mission:
Supporting students' physical and psychological health and activating
their role in serving their peers and community through volunteering
in health awareness activities.

Aims:
1. Promoting university students' physical and psychological health
   and well-being and raising their awareness, especially in sexual
   and reproductive health and psychosocial support.
2. Activating the role of colleges and relevant authorities in raising
   health awareness among students, especially health educators.
3. Spreading the culture of volunteering and community service.
4. Encouraging students to receive primary and consultative health
   care services and participate in raising health awareness among
   their families and communities.

Service Areas:
- Psychological counseling.
- Reproductive health.
- Healthy nutrition.

Clinic Activities:
- Fetal Monitoring Cardiotocography Workshop.
- Psychosocial Support Workshop.
- World Mental Health Day under the slogan:
  "Mental Health is a Priority for University Students".
