JUST Student Health Services

- Student health services are provided through the Health Care Center
  located in Irbid.
- Students pay health insurance fees during registration.
- The insurance covers services offered by the health center,
  except certain services such as plastic surgery and providing
  eyeglasses or hearing aids.
- Walk-in services are available 24 hours a day, seven days a week
  through general practitioners and clinics.
- Specialized clinics require appointments.

Clinic hours stated in the source:
- General medicine clinic: 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- Specialized clinics: 10:30 AM to 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM,
  Sunday through Thursday.

Hospitalization services are provided through:
- Princess Basma Teaching Hospital in Irbid.
- King Abdullah University Hospital (KAUH).

Source: JUST Student Life page.


JUST Health Center - About Us

Establishment:
JUST Health Center was established in 1986 when the university
first opened its doors.

Location:
The center is located opposite Prince Al-Hasan Youth Sport City
in Irbid. Transportation and parking are available.

The center provides low-cost primary healthcare services to
the communities of JUST University, Yarmouk University,
Al-Albayt University, and others.

Accreditation:
The center has maintained accreditation by the Health Care
Accreditation Council (HCAC) since 2011.

Medical Services

1. Preventive and Therapeutic Services:
- Family medicine clinics.
- Pediatric clinic.
- Well-baby clinic.
- Women's health and family planning clinic.
- Breast cancer screening clinic.
- Optometry clinic.
- Nutrition clinic.
- Smoking cessation clinic.

2. Diagnostic Services:

Diagnostic Laboratory:
- Biochemistry.
- Hematology.
- Microbiology.
- Urine and stool studies.
- Hormone and serology tests.

Diagnostic Radiology:
- X-ray.
- Ultrasound.
- Mammogram.

3. Pharmaceutical Services:
- Medication therapy management.
- Vaccines administered on site.
- Medication counseling.
- Smoking cessation support.

4. Training and Teaching Services:
- Family medicine residents.
- Fifth-year medical students.
- Pharmacy students.
- Nursing students.
- Medical laboratory students.
- Postgraduate students in different fields.

5. Community Health Services:
- Free medical days.
- Health awareness campaigns.
