JUST - Office of Arab, Foreign, and Minority Students

The office provides guidance and assistance to Arab, foreign, and minority students.

Services described in the source:
- Assistance with student inquiries and university transactions.
- Guidance and advice for students.
- Assistance with residence permits and visas.
- Assistance related to suitable student housing.
- Cultural, social, and legal services.
- Cultural and educational trips and activities.
- Communication with students and cultural attachés.

Location:
Deanship of Student Affairs building, ground floor, on campus.

Office hours:
The source indicates Sunday to Thursday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
Verify current working hours before providing them to students.
