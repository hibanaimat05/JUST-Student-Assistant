
JUST University Counseling Office and Services for Students
with Disabilities

Establishment:
The office was established in the 2004/2005 academic year.

General Description:
The office aims to provide comprehensive guidance to students,
with particular focus on students with disabilities.

Support Areas:
- Psychological support.
- Social support.
- Emotional support.
- Academic support.
- Family-related support.
- Economic support.

Counseling and Support Procedures:

The office addresses students' issues by:
1. Analyzing the causes of the student's issue.
2. Studying the student's background and circumstances.
3. Developing tailored counseling and support plans.
4. Customizing plans according to the severity of the issue,
   the student's social and psychological circumstances,
   and the degree of disability.
5. Finding effective solutions to reduce or resolve challenges
   or help students adapt to them.
6. Providing ongoing support and monitoring.

Office Working Mechanism

The University Counseling Office follows these procedures:

1. Receiving Students:
- Receiving students who seek counseling and listening to them.
- Building trust, safety, and confidentiality.

2. Coordination with Relevant Authorities:
The office establishes professional relationships with relevant
parties when necessary, including:
- The student's family.
- A psychiatrist.
- Admission and Registration.
- Academic advisors in departments.
- Student Services.
- Cultural and Social Activities.
- King Abdullah II Fund for Excellence Office.
- Other authorities that can help the student solve their problem.

3. Preparing Counseling Plans:
- Preparing an individual counseling and treatment plan.
- Considering each student's individual needs and circumstances.
- Including appropriate counseling methods in the plan.

4. Referral of Psychological Cases:
If the problem is psychological, the student is referred to the
Psychiatric Clinic at King Abdullah University Hospital,
in cooperation with the university student clinic.

5. Continuous Follow-up:
- Following up on cases continuously.
- Monitoring the student's improvement.
